# STRATEGY.md - Competitor Analysis, Positioning, Pricing and Keywords for Scoutline

Research snapshot verified September 2026. Prices and features re-checked against public pages on or near 2026-09-29.

## Who we compete with

### Contact databases and outbound platforms

- **Apollo**: All-in-one database plus sequences. Per-seat pricing. Verified 2026: Free $0, Basic $49/user/mo annual ($65 monthly), Professional $79 annual ($99 monthly), Organization $119 annual (3-seat minimum). Credit system (email 1 credit, phone 8). Large contact database. Third-party tests historically report email accuracy near 73 percent.
- **Clay**: Enrichment workflow builder with waterfall lookups across 100+ providers. Verified 2026: Free, Launch from ~$167-185/mo annual, Growth from ~$446-495/mo annual, Enterprise custom. Two credit types (Data Credits and Actions). Powerful but complex and cost climbs with volume. No native engagement layer.
- **Instantly / Smartlead**: Sending infrastructure and inbox rotation, roughly $30-50/mo range historically. They send emails but do not find the leads.
- **Lemlist**: Personalized multichannel outreach from about $79/user/mo including WhatsApp steps.
- **Enginy**: Positioned as all-in-one lead generation for agencies, credit based.
- **ZoomInfo, Cognism, Leadfeeder, UpLead, Hunter**: Enterprise data, EU-compliant data, visitor identification, verified B2B contacts, email finding.

### Lead-gen agencies

Belkins, CIENCE, Leadium and similar: managed outbound on retainer.

### Audit and prospecting widgets for agencies

- **SEOptimer**: Branded audits and embeddable audit widget. Verified 2026: DIY SEO from ~$29/mo, White Label ~$39/mo, White Label & Embedding ~$59/mo (annual discounts available). Most cited "audit as lead magnet" tool.
- Others: SE Ranking (Lead Generator widget), Search Atlas, Insites, MySiteAuditor, Sitechecker, WooRank, DashClicks, AgencyAnalytics, SEOcrawl, LemAudit, ConvertHook (AI-search visibility audit widget).

## What they do well

- Transparent, published pricing tiers.
- "Best for" positioning on every plan and every comparison table.
- Embeddable free tools that capture leads and create backlinks.
- Rapid time to value: a prospect audit in under 90 seconds.
- Heavy content operations: comparison pages, alternatives pages, and yearly "best tools" roundups, refreshed with the year in the title.

## Where they are weak (our opening)

- Databases sell lists. Everyone gets the same leads, so contacts are saturated.
- Nothing combines signal-based discovery + evidence + a ready proof asset + service matching + a message in one place.
- Per-seat pricing punishes agencies as they grow. Clay credits are hard to predict.
- Email accuracy from single-source databases forces users to buy separate verification tools.
- Audit widgets capture inbound leads from a tenant's own site but do not go find outbound leads.
- No mainstream tool detects "this lead is already served by another agency" and offers exclusivity.
- Almost nothing serves B2B agency-to-agency selling (AEO tools, white-label CRMs) with signal detection.

## Positioning

"Find agency clients that nobody else has pitched yet, with the proof already built."

Supporting claims (only publish claims we can demonstrate with our own data): fresh signals, evidence on every lead, a proof report per lead, flat workspace pricing with unlimited seats, worldwide coverage, exclusivity option.

## What we copy and what we beat

Copy: published pricing, "best for" tags, year-stamped refreshed content, embeddable free tools, comparison and alternatives pages, FAQ blocks.

Beat: original data (our own signal reports), evidence-first design, honest comparison tables that name competitor strengths (this builds trust and gets cited by AI answers), a genuinely useful free tool per hub, and proof from our own case study once the 30-day validation produces numbers.

## Pricing logic (defaults)

Flat per workspace, unlimited seats, credit based. One credit equals one fully enriched, scored, evidence-backed lead. Proof reports and messages are included in the plan quota.

- Trial: 14 days, 25 credits, no card required.
- Starter: 39 USD per month, 300 credits, 2 modes.
- Growth: 99 USD per month, 1,500 credits, all modes, follow-up sequences, integrations.
- Agency: 249 USD per month, 6,000 credits, white-label proof reports on the agency's own domain, public API, exclusivity claims, priority queue.
- Annual billing: two months free.
- Add-on credit packs, priced so margin stays healthy after LLM and infrastructure costs.

Gross margin target: at least 70 percent after LLM tokens, crawling compute, and storage. Real cost-per-lead calculation will be recorded here once Phase 1 infrastructure costs are known and after the first production pipeline run.
